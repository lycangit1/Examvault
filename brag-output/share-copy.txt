## 💼 LinkedIn (Institutional & Enterprise)
Paper leaks destroy student futures and cost examination boards hundreds of crores. 

Today, we're proud to unveil **ExamVault** — a zero-trust examination security gateway built from the ground up to prevent internal paper exfiltration before questions ever reach the printing press or CBT terminal.

Key Innovations:
1. **Least-Privilege RBAC & PostgreSQL RLS:** Question setters see only own drafts; reviewers see only assigned moderation queues.
2. **2-Man Authorization Lock:** Cryptographic package sealing requires two independent officers (Approver + Admin_2) to eliminate single-point insider compromise.
3. **Dynamic Session Watermarking & OCR Forensics:** Any photographed leak is trace-matched to the leaking session and device in under 3 seconds.
